## POST 1, the starter

```
# The Legacy of Goku II: Ascended

> The Legacy of Goku II as you remember it, expanded: Buu's Fury systems brought backwards, new transformations, new cutscenes, and the Future Trunks ending rebuilt as fights you actually play.

## The goal

This is the vanilla game, expanded. Not a remake, not a rebalance, not a difficulty patch.

Every map, every story beat and every fight you remember is still there and still plays the way it did. Nothing is cut. The work goes on top of it, in three directions:

**Bring Buu's Fury backwards.** Webfoot built better systems one game later: the ki pool, the HUD, the aura, the transformation flow, the boss bar. II is the same engine a year younger. Porting those back makes II play like the game it grew into instead of the rough draft of it.

**Finish what the game only showed you.** II ends its best story beat in a cutscene. Trunks goes back to his own time, and the game *shows* you Android 18, Android 17 and Cell being beaten. You never press a button. Those become real fights, on real maps, with real bosses.

**Give the cast what the show gave them.** Vegeta, Piccolo and Trunks reach forms the game never let them have, built the way the game's own transformations are built rather than bolted on.

More in the thread below. 🔽
```

## POST 2, the expansion

```
## What the expansion adds

### ⚔️ New battles
- The Future Trunks ending rebuilt: **18, 17 and Cell become real fights** instead of cutscenes, each on its own map
- A **second round against Cell** on a hand-made plateau map, with its own dialogue and its own ending
- **Seven Cell Jrs** in three waves instead of a scripted beating
- A **sparring fight, Gohan against Goku**, in the Capsule Corp training room

### ⚡ New transformations
- **Z+ for Vegeta, Piccolo and Trunks**: a grade above what the game allowed, each with its own icon, portrait and stat line
- **Super Saiyan 2 for Gohan**, with its stat bonus and its own message
- **Buu's Fury power-up aura**, gold or white by character, with ground dust
- The **ki bar fills during the transformation** instead of appearing full at the end

### 🎬 New cutscenes
- **Piccolo gives Gohan his gi** on Dende's Lookout, a new scene that explains an outfit the game never explains
- **Vegeta goes Super Saiyan** after his line to Android 19, and again against Super Perfect Cell
- A **new arena in the rain** at Capsule Corp, built for the Trunks and Cell fight
- A **custom game over card** if Cell beats you, instead of "You have died!"
- **Chapter title cards** at each chapter's story point
```

## Notes before posting

- **No ROM, no patch link.** A built ROM carries Webfoot's whole game. If the channel expects
  something playable, the normal form is a patch against a clean ROM, and that is a separate call.
- Credit where art came from. Buu's Fury art is Webfoot's own and is ported, not redrawn. Nothing
  here is taken from another modder's hack.
- A thumbnail matters. Every other project in that channel has one. A short clip of the Trunks
  versus Cell fight or the plateau round beats any paragraph above.
- Drop the emoji if the channel's style is plainer. They are there for scanability, not decoration.
