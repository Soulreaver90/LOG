Intro



Hello and welcome to you, the reader of this guide!  I am eternally
grateful (well, not really) to you for coming here. This is the Pokedex of
Dragon Ball Z. The fact that you are reading this means one of several things.
Either you want to learn more about the Dragon Ball Z characters, you, like me,
have no life, or you need help with this totally optional side quest.  Filling
the Capsule Corporation’s computer database has no reward other than a
permanent supply of DBZ info.  So read on, as I unveil The Legacy of Goku’s
data spectacular!!












The Scouter and its Functions/How this Guide Works

The scouter is obtained from Bulma at Capsule Corp. early on in the game.
It functions as a map and as an information gathering machine.  The information
is instantly sent back to the main computer at Capsule Corp. for future
reference.  Along with information about the character, it also provides stats
and a head shot. This guide will cover all the info you can gather so if you
missed one it doesn’t matter.  The info is listed as such:

     NAME
     HEALTH
     STRENGTH
     POWER
     ENDURANCE
     INFO
Some enemies such as snakes have multiple strengths, so they will have multiple
numbers in each category.  Info is listed in alphabetical order
