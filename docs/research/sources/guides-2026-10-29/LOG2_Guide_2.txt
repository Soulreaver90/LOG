===============================================================================
     3.1- Menu                                                           [A301]
===============================================================================

To access the menu, press the Start Button. You'll see the Status Screen in 

which you can see the level, health, experience and stats of your character.
The maximum level is level 50. Use the Directional Pad to switch character
information. Press the L Button to look at you journal. You'll see all the
quests you are doing. Press the Right Directional Button to see the quests that
you completed. Press R to see all the items you collected. Press R or L twice
to open the Option Menu.

===============================================================================
     3.3- The World Map                                                  [A303]
===============================================================================

In this screen you fly around above a world map. You'll see a little radar with
stars on it. Every star is a new location you can go. Press A when you are
above the entrance of an area to enter it.

===============================================================================
     3.4- Regular Screen                                                 [A304]
===============================================================================

In the top left corner you can see three bars. The red one represents your HP
or Hit Points. It can be refilled with meat. The Green bar decreases every time
you shoot a KI blast and is filled with the little yellow KI orbs you can find.
The Blue bar represents your Experience. To fill it up you have to defeat
enemies who walk around in the different areas. Next to that is a yellow box
which fills up slowly. Your character can turn Super Saiyan/Namek with it. The
second box shows if you can use KI and which blast you are using.

Except those things you'll notice many things when you walk around like flight
circles, Save Game Circles, breakable rocks among others. You'll also see gates
with numbers on it. Those gates show which level you have to have to enter.

===============================================================================
     3.5- Save game                                                      [A305]
===============================================================================

In some places in the game you'll find a round white circle with a blue C on
it. Go stand on it and press A. Now select Save. The game has now been saved.

===============================================================================
     3.6- Switch Characters                                              [A306]
===============================================================================

In some places in the game you'll find a round white circle with a blue C on
it. Go stand on it and press A. Now select Switch Characters. Choose the
character you want to use and press A. You'll now be using that character.

Outside Pepper Town
*******************************************************************************

Gohan tells you to destroy the boulder up ahead. So move forward and go to the

the next screen. Go right and there you'll have to fight your first boss,

Triceratops Jungle
*******************************************************************************

Break the green gate with the number 10 on it with Piccolo. You should level
up till a level between 15 and 20. Follow the path and go south when you reach
the first crossroads, you'll enter an area with a couple of lady bugs and
Mercenaries. In the next area you'll find a Golden Capsule and you can return
to the crossroad. Go over the bridge and go to the left to find Trunks' door.
But for now, you have to go to the right. Go down and defeat all the foes.
Proceed further down. After you defeat the Tiger Bandit you'll find a +1
Endurance Capsule. Go back to where all the foes were. Now go to the west when
you're there. Head down all the screens and follow the path over the water,
you'll find a Golden Capsule. Go back to the waterfall you saw. Go through it.
When you reach the fields, you have to watch out because there are
Triceratopses passing. You can talk to the people in the farm. Go to the south
and watch out for the Triceratopses.

Now the hard part begins. When you enter you see that you're dealing with a
labyrinth. You'll that three Triceratopses are coming towards you. Don't
worry, they won't hit you. They'll turn before they're with you. Follow them
and then hide in the hole in the crops until they come back and when they run
back to the entrance, you have to hurry to the next ally. But watch out. If
you stand there, another bunch of Triceratopses will hit you. So keep running
to the east and you'll enter a new screen. Your best bet in this screen is to
follow the Triceratopses that are running in the south of the screen. Follow
them till the next ally and then concentrate on the top Triceratopses. When
they run pass you, you have to hurry to the next ally. Follow that ally but
don't go into the next one, until you saw the Triceratopses pass. Follow them
until they go north, you have to go to the west then. Go to the right and then
take another ally to the right. You'll see a situation identical to the first
one now. Just follow the Triceratopses, hide in the hole and then continue
when they're going to the other side. Hide in the last ally, not the first.
After that you'll only have to pass one last group of Triceratopses. Continue
to the next screen where you'll see the Triceratops King.

Collect the Seven Dragon Balls
*******************************************************************************

The first Dragon Ball can be found in East District 439. Go to the Level 35
Gate for Goku which you saw when you where there with Vegeta to find the
Golden Capsules and the missing Namek. Once you are in the castle, go around
the pond and go to the north. You'll come in an area where you can go to the
north, the west and the east. Go to the East and the West. You'll see two
pumps. Press the A Button when you are standing in front of them. Now you have
to turn on three other statues. The first is located to the north of the
screen where you could go East or West to the pumps. Now go back to the room
with the pond and go to the East to disable the second statue and then to the
west to disable the third. Now go to the pond where the water is gone and go
down to pick up the Four Star Dragon Ball.
