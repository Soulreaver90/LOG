--------------------------
I. Future Trunks' Timeline
--------------------------

The story starts out in the Future Trunks' timeline playing as Future Trunks.
Here you get to see Future Gohan (as I will call him) and Future Trunks. 
After Gohan walks to the (N), follow him and attack the rock. Press A to do 
a meelee attack. You'll notice if you press A a couple of times, you can do 
combo attacks, something sorely lacking in Part 1 of this series. Follow 
Gohan (N) again and this time press B to shoot a Ki blast. Hit the rock twice
to destroy it. The ground shakes here and Gohan runs (N). It was the 2 
Androids (17 & 18) who caused that. They were attacking Pepper City. Head 
(N) and find Gohan. After almost going Super Saiyan and failing twice, Gohan
tells Trunks that he can fight with him but when Trunks turns his back, Gohan
knocks him out. When Trunks wakes up, it is darker. Stand on the raised 
portion (Flight Pad) and press A to fly to Pepper Town. Run around if you 
like and when you are ready to proceed with the story, head (N) and west a 
bit. You'll come across a sad scene where Androids 17 and 18 (out of view) 
pelt Gohan with energy blasts. Gohan dies, Trunks runs to him and finally 
feels the rage needed for him to complete the transformation to become a 
Super Saiyan. The screen fades to white and thats the end of that in the 
Future Timeline.

Cut to Goku's house in the Present Timeline.

----------------
II. Goku's House
----------------

Quests accessed: 

---Find the Mathbook

Chi Chi yaps to young Gohan about wanting him to be a lawyer blah blah, she
even thinks he can be PRESIDENT. President of the World (she said it, not 
me). haha They talk a bit and she tells Gohan to go find his Math book. This
is your very first Quest. Quests from now on that are accessed will be noted
as such, *new quest*. The Math book is upstairs. Head through the (E) door 
and go up the stairs. You'll see it. Current Quests/Completed Quests can be 
checked through the Status screen (press Start). Press L and it will show the
Journal screen. Press Right to see completed quests. Once you have the Math 
book, head back downstairs to the desk where you started. Gohan will start to
study and fall asleep.

When Gohan wakes up, the window will be open. Use that to get out of the 
house. 

----------------------
III. East District 439
----------------------

Quests accessed: 

---Meet Krillin and the others in the Northern Wastelands

Directly (N) of Goku's House is the first Save Point. If you are a fan of the
anime/manga, you know that the C on it stands for the Capsule Corporation.
Save if you like now, press A while standing on the save point. Later on you
can switch characters at save points.

Go towards the (NW). Make your way to the ledge and exit to the (W). You'll
find a +1 Power Capsule. Make your way back to where the Save point is and
head (NE) to the exit there. Dash (E) and head (N). You'll come across your
very first Character Gate. The floating number is what level the character 
has to be and the color of the number is a hint to who you need to be 
controlling in order to pass the Gate. This one is a no-brainer. Gohan's 
color is light blue. After you pass through the gate, you'll notice a second
one with a 2 floating over the Gate. You can guess what that means. ;) Head 
(E). You'll come across "Goku". Well, its not Goku, its a hallucination, 
sorta. The figure turns into final form Frieza and attacks you. If you can 
get away from him a tad, pepper him with Ki blasts. If you run out of Ki, 
punch and retreat. When you beat him, you reach Level 2. It was all a dream.
Make your way back to the Lv 2 character Gate after you talk to Krillen on 
the phone. The Saiyan Battle Armor Gohan spoke of to Krillen is in there. 
Head through the (N) exit and head out to the World Map. Time to fly!

-----------------------
IV. Northern Wastelands
-----------------------

Theres only one path you can take. Head (W) and use Ki blasts to take out the
flying Red Ribbon robots. There are 3 and when you destroy them all, the
barrier will disappear. Continue (N). There is a Namek (W) one screen from
where you are now, but there you need Goku to get to him, so come back here 
later. Also, if you go up the ramp and then (W) one screen, there is a
Character Gate, but its for Goku so you can't access it right now. Go back to
the ramp and head (N). Pass the skeleton and head (NW). There are two of the
Tiger Bandits here and one Ladybug. You'll find a Save point and a Flight Pad
at the (N)most part.

When you land, head down the ramp and grab the STR Capsule +1. Talk to 
everyone if you like, and when you're ready, head up the cliff where
Piccolo is waiting. Continue up the cliff top. Its on auto-pilot for a bit
here so sit back and watch the story. King Cold and Metal Frieza enter and
talk a bit. Frieza brings out some soldiers. Enter Future Trunks. Watch 
Future Trunks destroy them and their soldiers. Talk to your buddies if you 
want and use the Flight Pad on the left when you're ready.

Talk to Future Trunks and more auto-pilot story comes. Enter Goku. The 
dialogue is almost verbatum (word for word) from the show, especially when
Future Trunks takes Goku off to the side for their discussion. Still on 
auto-pilot. Read, read and read some more....

VI. West City
-------------

Quests accessed: 

---Get the Hercule Parade started to gain access to Piccolo
---Piccolo must talk to Goku or the Mayor about a problem in West City
--Get an open faced Club sandwich for Hercule
--Save the four missing children at West City Highway
--Find the album "Eyes of the Lion" and give it to Hercule


The Star on the World Map is where you need to go.

Goku says that he'll be waiting outside of Capsule Corporation for Piccolo 
and says that Bulma has something for you at Capsule Corporation. You are 
shown where Piccolo is and your Journal is updated with two new quests.

Speak with Hercule and you'll recieve Hercule's Autograph. He doesn't tell 
you directly, but he wants an "open faced Club sandwich". Head to the (N)most
area of this area and you'll see a kid walking around a tree. This is the 
Park and the kid wants Hercule's autograph. The kid gives you a Golden 
Capsule.

If you ever get lost, use the Red boxes around town, these are maps. There is
one in each section of town.

Head (W) one screen and go all the way to the (N) end of it. You'll notice an
exit to the (E). Exit to the (E) and you'll find a Golden Capsule. You'll 
need to talk to Dr. Brief at CC about it (CC will be what I call Capsule 
Corporation from now on). 

In the (NE)most part of town there is a guy that asks you to find 4 missing
children. *information is in the Quests section* To the (E) of the Save point
(N) of him is a secrety area. There is a Golden Capsule there.

---------------------------
VI.    West City
 A.    -Capsule Corporation
---------------------------

Quests accessed:

-See Bulma at Capsule Corporation
-Find the 25 Golden Capsules for Dr. Briefs
-Deliver Bulma's note to the Electronics store
-Deliver the Scouter Part to Bulma
-Investigate the Capsule Corporation espionage

CC is to the (E) of town. From the main entrance, if you head straight ahead
through the glass doors and make your way to the back of that area, you can 
train with your friends. If you lose, don't worry, you can't die, but you get
items if you win.

To get to Dr. Briefs easily without exploring, instead of entering in the 
main entrance, go (E) a bit more and enter there. He's walking back and forth
in the back of the lab. Bulma is on the 2nd floor. From the main entrance, 
take the (W) exit and go (S). Head up the stairs to the 2nd floor and you'll
come across Mrs. Briefs. Talk to her and she'll give you a Cookie, which 
restores Health. Continue to the back of the apartment and you'll find Bulma.
She gives you a note to take to the Electronics store. *new quest* On the way
to the Electronics store, stop into the house just (NW) of the entrance to 
CC. In the house, you'll find a STR Capsule +1. The Electronics store 
(Circuit Shack) is 3 screens (W) of the entrance to CC. Talk to Bucky in the
(NE) part of the store. Return to Bulma and you recieve the Scouter as a 
reward. The Scouter can be used to show a map of the current area you are in.
Press R to use it. You can also view info on people/enemies that are in range
of the Scouter. Press R to access the Scouter and then press L to switch to 
view any people or enemies in range. Move the Cursor and press A to view the
info. Any info you discover can be accessed by going to CC's central 
computer later, once you have access to it. Before you leave the room Bulma
is in, exit the room through the door to the (N) next to the TV. There is a
POW Capsule +1 out on the balcony.

When you go back downstairs to the Lobby area, the receptionist tells you 
that Dr. Briefs wants you to investigate a possible information leak in the 
company. Head (E) two screens and talk to the Guard. He gives you the Blue 
Key Card. This gives you access to two new areas. The Basement area and the 
East Tower on the 3rd floor. The Basement has a STR Capsule +3 and a 
Endurance Capsule +3. Now go to the East Tower on the 3rd floor and use the 
key card. Theres a Save point if you feel you'd like to use it. Go to the top
of the room and press the red switch. This unlocks the West Tower. Now go to
the West Tower. There is a man named Newman in there. He claims to be lost, 
but how can you get lost in an area that was sealed off? Hmm...theres a flash
of light and he summons an Wolf (400HP) that you can kill easily. After the 
battle, Newman confesses and you take him back to the Guard that gave you the
Blue Key Card. He gives you the Red Key Card as thanks. Now you can access 
the Computer Room which is one screen (W) from here. You can view all the 
info that you've gathered on people with the Scouter here.

This is all you can do at CC for now. Lets go get that open faced Club 
sandwich that Hercule wants so we can progress the story.

If you haven't done the quest "Save the four missing children at West City 
Highway", you have to do that in order to progress the story. Once you have
done that, go to the Newspaper stand. Clarence will give you a free Newspaper
for saving his son. Take the Newspaper and give it to Floyd at "Big Mouth" 
the restaurant. He gives you the open faced Club sandwich you need for 
Hercule. Now head (N) 2 screens and give the sandwich to Hercule. He whines 
about the music being wrong and he says he wants "Eyes of the Lion" to be 
playing. You can guess what this means....*new quest*. Head to the Record 
shop and talk to the guy. He doesn't have the album, and directs you to the 
Antique Shop, its at the top of the screen one screen (N) of the Record Shop.
Talk to Merv and he'll give it to you for free, because he can't imagine 
charging anyone for something SO BAD (guess that says something about 
Hercule, huh?). Head back to Hercule to complete the quest.

Now you can access Piccolo. *new quest* Step on the Save point and press A. 
You can switch to Piccolo now. Go talk to Goku in front of CC. Talking to the
Mayor in City Hall (N) of the Save point will give you a....*new quest*. He 
asks you to help a small town to the (S) from some Triceratops.

-----------------------
VII. Triceratops Jungle
-----------------------

Head (E) an bust through the Character Gate with Piccolo (you should still be
him after talking to the Mayor or Goku. If not, the closest Save point is in
front of CC where Goku was walking around.) Since Piccolo is Lv. 10 when you
get him, you can get right through the Gate. Get used to using your Scouter
to gather information on the enemies you fight from now on. It can be a 
lifesaver to know about how many HP the enemy has and how strong they are. 
The first enemy you can test it out on is the Kuma Mercenary in the screen 
after the Character Gate. Wait till you can see them in your screen and hit 
Select. Put the cursor on him and hit A. He has a STR of 17, and Piccolo only
has a STR of 12, so be careful. Head (S) and exit through the (W) exit. In 
the (SW) corner is a Golden Capsule Now you can cross the bridge. In the (N)
part of this area, there is a character gate for Trunks, make a note in yer 
head. Now exit to (S). Make your way (S)wards and you'll come across an area
where you can walk across moss or lillypads sitting on the top of the water.
Head all the way (E) and grab the Golden Capsule. Make your way back to the 
screen (N) where you came in at. Instead of going up the ramp to the higher 
area, go (NE) a bit and under the Waterfall. This will take you to the bridge
you crossed under on the way to the Golden Capsule. Cross the bridge and 
you'll be in the village. Talk to people to gather clues. You'll be needing 
to find the Triceratops King. The house in the (SE) corner has a chest 
outside with a STR Capsule +1.

Continue to the (S). This next area can kill you FAST if you aren't careful.
There are packs of 3 Triceratops walking around here. They have a pattern and
walk at the same speed all the time, so time it right. The exit is in the
(SE) corner. (If you haven't used the Scouter on a Triceratops yet, you can 
do it now if you like ;) ) There is only one more area to worry about and can
be tricky, but just take your time. You have to almost always DASH to make it
in time. Try not to get hit because theres a boss coming up real soon. The 
exit is in the (NE) corner. Time to fight the Triceratops King.

Scouter reading:

Triceratops King
HP: 850
STR: 21
POW: 1
END: 25

Use Piccolo's fast Ki attacks. When he charges you, dodge. He likes to attack
at an angle and he shakes a bit before he attacks, so time your dodge 
accordingly. There are 3 rocks at the top of the battlefield that you can 
break for health/Ki. I don't know if its random, but I got a Roasted Chicken
and 2 small Ki orbs. Beat him and he'll agree that the triceratops will live
in peace with the humans. You are transported back to the middle of the 
village and a Flight Pad appears. Use the Flight Pad and it takes you back to
near the entrance to West City. Head (N) and you'll encounter someone who 
looks familiar, but you haven't seen him before....enter COOLER! He wants to
fight Goku, and will be waiting for him on New Namek. *make a mental note*

-----------------
VIII. West City
   A. Warlord's Domain
-----------------

Quests accessed:

---Retrieve the City Key and return it to the Mayor
  -Rescue the man kidnapped by the warlord

Go to see the Mayor at City Hall. You walk in on a ceremony where the Mayor 
is giving the key to the city to Hercule and someone steals it. *new quest*

You'll see where the man who stole the key goes, just follow him. As you're 
following him, stop by the grouping of 3 houses and talk to the red haired 
lady standing outside to receive a new quest, which is to find her husband 
who was kidnapped by the Warlord. Now, back to chasing the culprit. He'll 
end up running to the Warlord's Domain. The guards will open the door for 
you. There is a Save point for you if you want to use it. Exit to the (W). 
If you look at your Scouter (Press R), you'll see a cutout area in the (SW) 
part of this area. There is a Golden Capsule there. Now exit to the (W) but 
be careful as you head that way...there is a Ladybug that will hit you if 
you're not careful and walk so fast that you don't see it. 

In this next area there are 2 exits, one to the (N) and one to the (SW). 
There is a cave next to the (N) exit that is sealed off by some kind of 
magic. Take the (N) exit and you'll see the worn down Warlord's hideout. 
Before entering, go to the right side of the building and make your way 
around the building Counter-clockwise. You'll find a Kidnapped Man on the (W)
side of the screen. Talk to him and he'll give you a Golden Capsule. Now 
double back and enter the gate of the hideout and on the right side, you'll 
find a STR Capsule +1.

Taking the (SW) exit, you can exit to the (N) or to the (W). They both lead 
to the same screen. With the (N) exit, you'll have another screen to go 
through. Continue (W). Here is an area with a beach. On the beach near the 
dock you'll find a STR Capsule +1. There is a Flight Pad at the (N) of this 
area (noted by the orange dot on your Scouter). When you land, you'll find a
house to the (N). Enter and there is an END Capsule +3 in the chest by the 
door. This house belongs to the guy who stole the key to the city. His name 
is Robert and he stole the key for a good reason. He says that Hercule 
doesn't deserve it (which is true, haha). Robert says Hercule doesn't deserve
it because Hercule claims to have beaten the Warlord, but Robert is the one 
that beat the Warlord. Robert left the key in a cave, and put a spell on it.
Return to the cave that was sealed by the magic that you saw earlier. The key
is in a chest in the back of the cave. Now go back to the Mayor in City Hall.

XI. Amenbo Island
-----------------

Quests accessed:

---Meet up with the others on the Southern Continent
-Find the seven missing Nameks and return them to Capsule Corporation

You'll find the whole DBZ crew here (minus Chiaotzu). Talk to everyone if you
like. Make sure to speak with Yajirobe, he'll give you another Senzu Bean. 
Move (W) onto the cliff and auto-pilot takes over. Listen to them talk and
hit the Flight Pad which takes you to Sasebo, the city on Amenbo Island. Just
follow the road and Yamcha yells that he found them. Unfortunately, he's now
unconscious on the ground. Time to meed Androids 19 and 20. 19 is the fat 
white one, 20 is the old guy. The rest of the team joins you and Krillin 
takes Yamcha to Bulma to get a Senzu Bean. Goku wants to fight in a place 
where there are no innocent people. 20 agrees that it is too crowded and 19 
destroys the surroundings, which kills all people on the island (minus the Z
team and the Androids of course) They all fly off (N).

Meanwhile...

Vegeta is training deep in space on an asteroid. You then get to control him.
He can go Super Saiyan now. Use L to scroll through the special attacks, and
choose the Z icon. Press B to start the transformation. Its a long 
transformation so if any enemies are near you, try to block them off with a
rock or a tree so you can pull it off. You can disable SS by pressing B 
again. SS uses EP, so watch your gauge.

Enter the space ship and you'll be transported to CC. Exit to the (E) and a
scientist from CC asks you to keep an eye out for the 7 missing Nameks. They
want to send them back to New Namek to be reunited with their families. Now
use the World Map and fly to the Southern Continent to meet up with the Z 
team. 

Its not really necessary to do this, but in order to complete the "Train with
Roshi" quest, you have to. Go to Master Roshi's Island and "train" with him.
Talk to Roshi and Vegeta will learn the "Two-handed Smash" attack.

-----------------------
XII. Southern Continent
-----------------------

Quests accessed:

---Locate Dr. Gero's lab in the Northern Mountains


When you land, head (N). Just a few steps and a cutscene ensues. Android 20 
states that this is where they will be fighting. Goku wants to know how the 
Androids knew their names. And coughs (the heart virus has started to 
attack). Talk, talk, talk....read, read, read...*yawn*. Goku turns Super 
Saiyan. 19 and 20 don't expect it, but they don't think its any cause for 
worry. After the talking is done, you gain control of Vegeta again. There are
purple Tiger Bandits coming after you now. They have 325 HP so they're tough,
be careful.

There is a Save point nearby if you want to save now. From the starting 
screen, take the (NE) exit. There are 2 Tiger Bandits in this screen. Head 
(E) and if you take the (S) exit, you'll find the Save point. Head back up a
screen from the Save point and there is a Lv. 15 Character Gate for Gohan in
the screen to the (E). Make a note of that for later, its on your Scouter but
you need to remember that its there. From the Character Gate, go back a 
screen to the (W). Going (N) takes you to a screen with about 5 or 6 
Ladybugs. There is an electric gate that goes away when you destroy the 
Ladybugs. (Most  enemies respawn when you leave a screen and come back. Note
that the Ladybugs here don't respawn) There is a little more of the level to
explore to the (W), but nothing of interest. If you want to explore there, 
your choice. The first screen to the (W) from the electric gate has 2 
Ladybugs. The next screen has a couple Tiger Bandits and a Ladybug. The last
unexplored screen has 2 Tiger Bandits. You can always use more EXP, so go for
it. When you're ready, head through the disabled electric gate to the (N). 
Take either exit, they go to the same place. Head (W) and make your way to 
the end for a Gold Capsule. Double back and head (N) to find Goku fighting 
19. Goku is dominating 19 for a bit, and then the virus starts taking its 
toll on him. He loses power, and the gang figures its the heart virus that 
Future Trunks told them about. Vegeta enters and gives one of his patented 
Veggie speeches on how worthless someone (ie. Goku) is. Yamcha takes Goku 
home to get him started on the Antidote. Time to fight 19.

Scouter reading:

Android 19
HP: 2970
STR: 21
POW: 14
END: 11

DO NOT use Ki blasts (Ki blasts or Big Bang) on him. That will heal him, and
with near 3000 HP, you don't want to give him any edge over you. Go SS ASAP 
and pummel him with punches. If (WHEN) you need health/energy, there are 
rocks scattered around the edges of the battlefield. If you get out of 
punching range from 19, he'll start throwing energy blasts at you in quite 
rapid succession. So stay semi-close to him. If you have the Two-Handed Smash
Special Meelee attack for Veggie from Master Roshi's "training", that is a 
good thing to use on 19. My Vegeta was Level 21 at the time that I fought 19
and the THS did about 150 damage which is great, since my regular punches 
were only doing about 60-65 with criticals doing about 125). As long as you 
don't take too much damage, this battle will fly by....you have 2 Senzu Beans
in reserves, unless you used them already.

After the battle, Vegeta flies over to where 20 is standing on a ledge. The 
usual smack talk ensues (this IS Vegeta, after all) and he destroys the ledge
20 was standing on. 20 loses his hat in the explosion and escapes (N), 
running like a dog with his tail between his legs. You control Piccolo now, 
head (N) and there is a Save point where you cah change characters if you 
like, I like Vegeta since he can go SS. Continue (N). Snake your way (N) and
take the Flight Pad. BOSS FIGHT!!

Scouter reading:

Android 20
HP: 2810
STR: 24
POW: 19
END: 14


Same tactics as 19 really. The only difference is 19 has/had 160 HP more. 
There is a lot more room to move around. There are only 4 rocks around the 
arena to break, so use sparingly. When he gets down to about 1/5 health, the
battle is over.

The gang runs in and Future Trunks arrives. Vegeta now knows that Trunks is 
his son. Talking, more talking. Read. :p After all that is done, a World Map
appears and everyone flies off to try to find Dr. Gero's lab. Exit using the
map.

------------------------
XIII. Northern Mountains
------------------------

Quests accessed:

---Follow Androids 16, 17, and 18
--Destroy the three power generators to drop Dr. Gero's barrier

From the first area, save if you want, I'm sure you will after that battle 
with 20. Head (S) and kill the Warthogs and collect your Golden Capsule. 
Backtrack to the save point. To the North is a Lv. 25 Gohan Character Gate. 
If your Gohan isn't to that level yet, make a note on that one as well. We 
only have one way left to go so go (W) from the Save point. Make your way (N)
next. Finally a new enemy and not just a pallette swap on old ones(besides 
the Warthog), a Hawk. Get past them and there are a few Warthogs. There is a
Lv. 30 Piccolo Character Gate here to the (N) a tad. If he's the right level,
go back to the Save point and switch out if you aren't playing as him 
already. If not....make ANOTHER mental note.  Continue on (W). The next 
area has 3 Hawks, head straight on through...only one way to go anyway. The 
next screen has a POW Capsule +3 to the left. Continue (N)wards to run into 
Dr. Gero (20).

Scouter reading:

Android 20
HP: 1900
STR: 25
POW: 21
END: 17

He's at full health, but you'll note that he only has 1900 HP this time 
around instead of 2810 HP. Almost 1000 less than last time. Good. The best 
way I found to kick his @$$ is to get above him ( (N) side of the screen) and
get into a pattern of punching with a pattern while rushing him. Do that 
about twice and he'll run away. Follow him (N). Save point. To the (W) is a 
Character Gate for Goku. I don't know what level is required, since I don't 
have Goku yet. Mental note, again. Go back (E) and head (N) from the Save 
point.

Here you have to destroy the 3 generators powering the barrier to Dr. Gero's
lab. Beware the Eggbots, they like to smack you with those little hydraulic 
arms of theirs but stand back a tad until they explode. Touching them after 
they're "dead" but before they blow up will damage you. Head (E) and destroy
the orange parts on the generator. This one disables the middle laser 
barrier.

Dr. Gero again. Use the aforementioned tactic on him, getting into a rythm of
hitting him and knocking him back so you can hit him, but he can't hit you.
When he has about half of his life left, he'll flee (N). You can fight him 
again by going back to the screen where the barrier is. You might want to 
continue (E) after the 1st Gero battle, but we'll come back to that later.
