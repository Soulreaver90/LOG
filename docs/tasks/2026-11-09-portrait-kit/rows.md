| **A dialog portrait's backdrop is a RADIAL SPOTLIGHT, not a flat colour and not a diagonal ramp.** It is brightest in the middle and falls off in rings through a short palette ramp, with an ordered dither between each pair of bands. Measured over the 89 vanilla dialog portraits that unpack to a full 64x64: taking only pixels that at least 75 percent of a backdrop group share, which is background because the characters differ and the backdrop does not, a radius model fits 95 percent of them with 18 levels while an `x + y` diagonal fits 96 percent with 99 levels. **The diagonal scores as well only because it has five times the free parameters**, and it paints a diagonal band that is in no real portrait.
