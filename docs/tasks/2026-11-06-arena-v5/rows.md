| **The two appearance handlers read their table base from TWO DIFFERENT literals, and in our build those literals hold different tables.** `appearance_of_set @0x080042BC` (set id to entry index, the ext 73 path) loads its base with `LDR r4,[pc,#0x30]` at `@0x080042BE`, which resolves to `rom:0x42F0`. Vanilla `appearance_revert @0x08003ED4` (the flags and revert read, the ext 70 path) loads its base with `LDR r4,[pc,#0x274]` at `@0x08003ED6`, which resolves to `rom:0x414C`. In the arena build `gohan_gi` points `rom:0x42F0` at its 22 entry copy `rom:0x800000`, and `rom:0x414C` holds `ssj2_form`'s 23 entry table `rom:0x88DC54`. **So a tool that maps a set to an entry and then reads that entry's flags must resolve EACH table from its OWN literal.** Reading one literal for both jobs is right only while the two tables agree, and they already differ in length | #form #script #tool | C (both handlers read from the disassembly, both literals read from the built ROM) | AR5 |
| **Caveat on the row above, and it is the live state: `rom:0x414C` is NOT the word the live revert reads.** In the arena build `rom:0x3ED4` holds `00 4b 18 47` (`ldr r3,[pc,#0] ; bx r3`) and the literal `07 a9 89 08`, so `appearance_revert` is REPLACED by `fullpower_ssj`'s hook at `rom:0x89A906` (row 555). That hook loads its own base with `LDR r4,[pc,#0xA0]` at `@0x0889A910`, resolving to `rom:0x89A9B4`, which holds `0x0888DC54`. **Same value as `rom:0x414C`, different word.** So reading `rom:0x414C` gives the right table today and would go stale the day the hook's pool moves. The live chain to follow is `rom:0x3ED4` to the hook to `rom:0x89A9B4` | #form #script #tool | C (bytes at `rom:0x3ED4`, `rom:0x89A9B4` and `rom:0x414C` in the built ROM) | AR5 |
| **`fullpower_ssj`'s revert hook SKIPS appearance entries 5 and 20 under its own condition.** The body at `rom:0x89A906` holds `00 2e 03 d0` (`CMP r6,#0 ; BEQ`), then `05 2d 0a d0` (`CMP r5,#5 ; BEQ`) and `14 2d 08 d0` (`CMP r5,#0x14 ; BEQ`), where r5 is the record's appearance entry index. Entry 5 is Gohan's gi Super Saiyan and entry 20 is Goku's Super Saiyan.
